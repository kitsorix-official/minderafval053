# MinderAfval053

> An independent, open-source Dutch civic information platform providing transparent waste cost calculations and practical guidance for the Diftar (differentiated waste tariffs) system introduced in Enschede, Netherlands in 2026.

## Overview

MinderAfval053 is a citizen-initiated, non-governmental website dedicated to making the new Diftar waste management policy in Enschede understandable and financially transparent for all residents. The platform translates complex municipal policy documents and tariff structures into clear, personalized insights. It is **not** affiliated with the Municipality of Enschede or Twente Milieu.

## What is Diftar?

Diftar (Gedifferentieerde Tarieven, meaning "Differentiated Tariffs") is a waste management pricing system where residents pay a fixed annual base fee (vastrecht) plus a variable fee each time their residual waste (restafval) is collected. The system is designed to incentivize better waste separation: the more you recycle (GFT organic waste, PMD plastics, paper), the less residual waste you produce and the lower your costs.

Key principles:
- Every household pays a fixed annual base tariff (approximately EUR 264.84 in 2026)
- Variable costs per residual waste collection vary by container size (EUR 4.50 for 140L, EUR 7.50 for 240L)
- Underground containers cost EUR 1.50 per 30L deposit
- GFT (organic waste), PMD (plastic packaging), paper, and glass collection remain free
- Better waste separation directly reduces annual costs

## Key Features

### Waste Cost Calculator (Rekentool)
The core feature of MinderAfval053 is an interactive calculator that allows residents to estimate their annual waste costs based on:
- Container size (140L or 240L)
- Collection frequency (0-26 times per year)
- Whether they use underground containers
- Household size and waste separation behavior

### Tariff Overview 2026
Complete breakdown of all waste tariffs for Enschede in 2026, including:
- Fixed base tariff: EUR 264.84 per year
- 140L residual waste container: EUR 4.50 per collection
- 240L residual waste container: EUR 7.50 per collection
- Underground container: EUR 1.50 per 30L deposit
- Example cost scenarios for small (1-2 persons), average (3-4 persons), and large (5+ persons) households

### How Diftar Works (5-Step Explanation)
An interactive walkthrough explaining the Diftar system in 5 clear steps:
1. Fixed base tariff
2. Container size selection
3. Cost per collection
4. Waste separation benefits
5. Potential savings

2. Flattening packaging (up to EUR 30/year savings)

4. Textile to textile containers (up to EUR 15/year savings)
5. Conscious shopping (up to EUR 20/year savings)

### Bulky Waste (Grof Afval)
Information about disposal of large items:

- EUR 0.16 per extra kg after quota for bulky waste
- EUR 0.10 per kg for rubble (no free quota)
- Free unlimited disposal for electronics and small chemical waste
- Three recycling center locations in Enschede with opening hours
- Milieupas (environmental pass) required for access

## Key Terms

- **Diftar** - Gedifferentieerde Tarieven (Differentiated Tariffs) - pay-as-you-throw waste system
- **Restafval** - Residual/general waste (gray bin)
- **GFT** - Groente, Fruit en Tuinafval (vegetable, fruit and garden waste - green bin)
- **PMD** - Plastic verpakkingen, Metalen verpakkingen, Drinkverpakkingen (plastic, metal, drink cartons)
- **Vastrecht** - Fixed annual right/fee for waste collection
- **Lediging** - Collection/emptying of a waste container
- **Milieupas** - Environmental access card for recycling centers
- **Milieupark** - Recycling center / environmental park
- **Kwijtschelding** - Remission/exemption of fees
- **Kliko** - Dutch brand name commonly used for wheelie bins
- **Ondergrondse container** - Underground waste container (for apartments/high-rise)
- **Inworp** - Deposit/drop-off of waste into an underground container
- **Stortquotum** - Free disposal quota at recycling centers
- **GBTwente** - Gemeentelijk Belastingkantoor Twente (municipal tax office)
- **Twente Milieu** - Regional waste management organization
